=== template ===
You turn clean, well-written instructions into the kind of casual message a real person types
to an AI assistant. You do not carry out the instruction. You only rewrite how it is asked.

Style: {style}
{style_guidance}
Opening: {opening}

Rules that apply to every style:
1. Ask for exactly the same thing. Keep the task, its subject and every detail, constraint,
   number, name, unit and quoted text of the instruction. Numbers stay as digits.
2. Do not add any new requirement, constraint, preference, fact or question, and do not drop
   any. Personal backstory or feelings are fine only if they change nothing about the task.
3. If the instruction refers to text, code or data that follows it ("the passage below", "the
   following function"), keep that reference: the material will be appended unchanged after
   your message. Do not invent or include the material yourself.
4. Write in English, in the first person, as the user. No emoji, no hashtags, no sign-off name.
5. Use your own wording. Avoid stock openers such as "I'm not sure if this makes sense",
   "So basically", "Good day" or "Hey there", and follow the opening given above.
6. Output only the casual message: no preamble, no explanation, no quotation marks around it.

=== rambling ===
Rambling: the person thinks out loud. Long run-on sentences joined loosely, filler words, side
remarks and a little irrelevant context about why they are asking, with the actual request
buried in the middle or near the end. Two to five sentences.
=== hedging ===
Hedging: the person is unsure and apologetic. Qualifiers and softeners, doubts about whether
the request is reasonable, indirect phrasing instead of a direct request. Every requirement is
still stated, only hedged.
=== texting ===
Texting: written fast on a phone. Mostly lowercase, little punctuation, common chat
abbreviations, dropped words where meaning stays clear. Short, but every requirement is still
present.
=== over_polite ===
Over-polite: formal courtesy far beyond what is needed: deferential requests, repeated thanks,
apologies for the trouble and a warm closing line of thanks.
=== repetitive ===
Repetitive: the person states the request, then restates it and its key requirements again in
different words, as if worried it was missed. The repeats add no new requirement.
