**C5.4** (Bayes optimal no worse than MAP on validation accuracy, noise 0.1): difference -0.008, **fail** (smallest difference over the noise sweep -0.008).

**Reading.** The Bayes optimal classifier is optimal only *in expectation under its own model*:
labels produced by one hypothesis in H with independent label noise, and a prior that
matches. Neither holds here. The Phase 1 version spaces are empty in every category (C1.3), so
no conjunction explains the judge labels, and the "noise" is systematic (whole prompts
degrade). On about 100 training pairs per category the posterior is concentrated: the MAP
hypothesis alone holds 16-53% of the mass, and the vote mostly repeats it. Where it differs,
it adds hypotheses that fit the training part by chance, which costs 10 of 1,250 validation
predictions at noise 0.1 (the difference is 0 at noise 0.2). Both rules are below the
majority-class baseline (pooled 0.722), so the conjunctive language is too weak for this
task, which is the same finding as C1.3. C5.4 is recorded as failed; the gap is small and of
the size expected from posterior mass on chance-fitting hypotheses, not an implementation
error (the classifier matches the hand-computed examples in `tests/`).
