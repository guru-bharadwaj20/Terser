## 8.2.3 Max vs average pooling (3 seeds)

| pooling | prompt_auc | prompt_auc_sd | token_f1 | token_f1_sd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| avg | 0.7477 | 0.001563 | 0.6759 | 0.001874 |
| concat | 0.7549 | 0.000694 | 0.6765 | 0.002808 |
| max | 0.7526 | 0.002815 | 0.6763 | 0.003054 |

Max pooling asks *is there any droppable region* (one strong filler n-gram fires the head); average pooling asks *how much of the prompt is droppable*. Concatenation lets the head use both; in the table above it has the highest prompt-head AUC and average pooling the lowest (an earlier hard-coded sentence here credited average pooling).
