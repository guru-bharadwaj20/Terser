## C9.1 Tabular convergence

Best learner (alpha=0.1, gamma=0.99, decay=0.999): max |dQ| over the last 1,000 episodes = **2.74e+00** (target < 1e-3: **fail**).

**Why C9.1 fails.** The state `(filler, constraint, length buckets, category, ops applied)` lumps
many different prompts together. After the same action in the same state, the STOP reward
depends on *which* prompt was drawn, swinging between about +40 (savings) and about -100 (gate
says degraded). The environment is therefore stochastic at the table level. With a constant
alpha the Q-values track a moving average of these samples and never settle (max |dQ| ≈ alpha
x reward spread). Annealing epsilon to 0 helps (|dQ| about 1 at alpha 0.05-0.1) but cannot
remove reward noise from the aliased states. Reaching 1e-3 needs a decaying step size
(alpha_t = 1 / N(s, a), Robbins-Monro) or a richer state. Recorded as a follow-up.
